    Intelligence with a human checkpoint.
    VYOM+ extracts invoice data, normalizes it, and checks the internal consistency of GST identifiers, tax splits, and totals.
    Local first, with honest limits
    Digital documents and spreadsheets use local parsers. Images and scanned PDFs can use local OCR if it is installed. Handwritten documents can use an optional, configured local vision-language model; otherwise they fall back to OCR.
    Checking the available extraction tools…
    Review is part of the process
    Low confidence always needs human review. A reviewer confirmation records your check; it does not guarantee the extraction is correct or erase validation findings. Always compare critical fields with the source.
    Validation is a consistency check, not GST legal certification, tax advice, or an accountant’s sign-off. Displayed totals are descriptive only.
